# Contoso Music Festival: Guest Guide

The Contoso Music Festival is a fictional three-day music and food festival. This guide is the
evidence the Guest Assistant is allowed to use. Anything that isn't written here
is something the guide does not say.

## Entry and wristbands

- Gates A and B open at 12:00 on all three days. Gate A is the main entrance next
  to the Info Desk. Gate B is the east entrance, next to the rideshare pickup and
  the campsite.
- A lost wristband can be replaced at the Info Desk with photo ID and proof of
  purchase. There is no fee.
- Guests can leave and re-enter once per day, through Gate A only, with their
  wristband.
- Bags must be clear and no larger than A4, or a small belt bag. Glass, drones, and
  professional cameras (lenses over 100 mm) are not allowed. Empty reusable water
  bottles are allowed.

## Getting here

- City shuttles leave every 20 minutes from 11:00 until 01:00.
- Lot A is general parking. Lot C is for blue badge holders and is the closest lot
  to Gate A.
- Rideshare pickup and drop-off is in the Gate B east lot.

## Food and drink

- The festival is cashless. Cards and phones only, and there are no ATMs on site.
- Free water refill stations are at the Main Stage, the Food Court, the campsite,
  and Gate A.
- Every food stall displays its allergen information. Guests can also ask the
  stall staff.

## Help on site

- The Medical Tent is next to the Lantern Stage and is open 24 hours. Sunscreen
  is free there.
- Free earplugs are available at the Info Desk.
- Lost and found is at the Info Desk by Gate A, open from 11:00 to midnight.
- If a child is separated from a family, tell any staff member in a yellow vest.
  Free child wristbands with a parent's phone number are available at the Info
  Desk before entry.
- Phone charging lockers at the Info Desk cost 5 euros per hour.

## Accessibility

- The accessible viewing platform is at Main Stage left, with a step-free ramp
  from Gate A.
- A quiet sensory room is next to the Medical Tent.
- Assistance dogs are welcome. Other pets are not allowed.

## Weather

- If lightning is within 10 km, performances pause and a siren sounds. Staff guide
  guests to the Food Court hall for shelter.
- Ticket refunds are only offered if organizers cancel a whole festival day. Line-up
  changes and weather pauses don't qualify.

## Camping

- Quiet hours are 23:00 to 07:00.
- Open flames and campfires are not allowed.
- Showers are hot between 07:00 and 10:00 only.
